Our oils

What are cold-pressed (wood-pressed) oils?

Cold-pressed oils are extracted by slowly crushing seeds without high heat or chemical solvents. We use a traditional wooden ghani — called gaanuga in Telugu and mara chekku in Tamil — which turns slowly so the oil stays cool and keeps its natural aroma, colour and taste. The oil is then left to settle naturally and filtered. It is never refined, bleached or deodorised.

How are cold-pressed oils different from refined oils?

Most supermarket oils are extracted with high-speed expellers and solvents such as hexane, then refined, bleached and deodorised at high temperatures. Cold-pressed oils skip all of that, so they keep the natural colour, aroma and character of the seed.

Which oils and products do you sell?

Cold-pressed groundnut, white sesame (gingelly), black sesame, coconut, mustard (kachi ghani), sunflower and castor oils, plus pure ghee and natural honey.

Why is there some sediment at the bottom of the bottle?

Cold-pressed oils are unrefined, so a little natural sediment can settle over time. It is normal and a sign that the oil has not been chemically processed. Store the bottle upright and pour gently.

Care & storage

How should I store cold-pressed oils?

Keep them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and close the cap tightly after use. Coconut oil and ghee may turn solid in cooler weather — that is natural; stand the bottle in warm water to liquefy.

My honey has crystallised. Is it still good?

Yes. Natural honey can crystallise over time. Stand the jar in warm (not boiling) water for a while and it turns runny again.
