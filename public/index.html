Questions

Good to know

What are cold-pressed (wood-pressed) oils?

Cold-pressed oils are extracted by slowly crushing seeds without high heat or chemical solvents. We use a traditional wooden ghani — called gaanuga in Telugu and mara chekku in Tamil — which turns slowly so the oil stays cool and keeps its natural aroma, colour and taste. The oil is then left to settle naturally and filtered. It is never refined, bleached or deodorised.
