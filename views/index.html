The ghani way

From seed to bottle, slowly

  1. 1

    Hand-picked seeds

    Groundnuts, sesame, coconut and mustard are cleaned and sorted before pressing.

  2. 2

    Pressed cold in a wooden ghani

    The wooden ghani turns slowly, so the oil stays cool and keeps its natural aroma.

  3. 3

    Settled & bottled

    The oil rests, settles naturally and is filtered — never refined, bleached or deodorised.
