Know your oil

Wood-pressed vs. industrial refined

Most supermarket oils are refined. Here is what is different about a traditional wood-pressed oil.

 Varam wood-pressedIndustrial refined
ExtractionSlow pressing in a wooden ghaniHigh-speed expellers and chemical solvents (usually hexane)
HeatKept low during pressingHigh temperatures during extraction and refining
After pressingLeft to settle naturally, then filteredBleached and deodorised
Colour & aromaNatural colour and aroma of the seedPale and largely odourless
SedimentA little natural sediment — a sign it is unrefinedFully clarified
